THE IDEA

A better question than "How many calories?"

EvenPlate helps you look at a meal through protein, fiber, fat, and volume, then reflect on your own experience. A practical starting point for building plates that feel good to you.

01 / NOTICE
☷

Scan what is there.

Photograph a meal to see a relative fullness estimate, a plain-language explanation, and ideas for what you could add.

02 / EXPLORE
✚

Build what is next.

Choose foods or describe your own. Try a different combination and assess the whole plate before you save it.

03 / LEARN
●

Find your rhythm.

Keep a meal diary, check in with your energy, and notice patterns over time without turning eating into a spreadsheet.

THE EVENPLATE PRINCIPLE

Food is more than a number.

EvenPlate offers educational estimates, not measured nutrition facts or medical advice. Your appetite, body, and circumstances matter. Use the app as a prompt to pay attention, not a rulebook.
